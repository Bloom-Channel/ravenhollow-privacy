Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Ravenhollow Games ("we", "our", or "us") built Shadows of Ravenhollow as a free game. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our game.

Information We Collect

Shadows of Ravenhollow does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The game runs entirely on your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

The game saves your progress locally on your device using Unity's PlayerPrefs system. This includes:

  • Game progress (completed scenes, star ratings)
  • Audio settings (music, sound effects, and voice preferences)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

The game does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Our game does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
